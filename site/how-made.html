Behind the captures

How these images and videos were made

The interface is Chimera's actual React components and app state, rendered in a local browser. The Atlas project, people, tasks, notes and resource figures are fictional.

Product images

The screenshots render the production screens with a local fixture that supplies their data. They show the interface without connecting to personal accounts, a live daemon or a provider. Secret entries contain names and masked states only. The homepage hero uses the full workspace image as its video poster; its film moves into the context handoff.

The desktop monitor is a real component. Its Atlas pricing preview is a synthetic SVG drawn by the fixture; ownership, actions and status are scripted. No desktop was captured or controlled.

Product videos

The films use real clicks, keystrokes and focus changes in those components. A scripted fixture answers RPC requests and replays task states; the films do not record agents executing work.

Camera moves, pointer motion, captions and timing are editorial. Each film is silent and sampled at 15 frames per second. Durations and resource figures are not performance measurements.

Playback starts when you choose it. Native controls, English captions, static posters and text steps remain available without JavaScript. Expand the player to inspect small interface text, or use the static alternatives for reduced motion.
